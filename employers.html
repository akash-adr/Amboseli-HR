How the APA Hiring Process Works

A streamlined journey from client requirements to final candidate selection

STEP 1

CLIENT INITIATION

Clients / Candidates approach APA to begin the hiring process.

Details:

  • Job Description
  • Recommended Qualifications
  • Required Experience
  • Company Profile
  • Work Environment
  • Career Prospects & Perks
STEP 2

CANDIDATE SOURCING

APA begins sourcing potential candidates.

Details:

  • Uses in-house database
  • Identifies professionals from relevant companies
  • Posts job advertisements (if required)
  • Connects with potential candidates
STEP 3

INITIAL SCREENING

APA evaluates candidate suitability.

Details:

  • Conducts initial discussions
  • Assesses candidate fit
  • Decides progression to next stage
  • Collects resumes
STEP 4

EVALUATION & SHORTLISTING

APA performs detailed evaluation.

Details:

  • Reviews resumes from database & applications
  • Shortlists qualified candidates
  • Explains client requirements
  • Gets candidate approval to share profiles
STEP 5

FINAL SUBMISSION

APA submits candidates to the client.

Details:

  • Shares resumes with client
  • Provides candidate assessments
  • Client proceeds with selection process
