About Us

Who We Are

We are an established Human Resource services & Recruitment & Staffing company designed to provide human resource packages to the specific needs of a few selected clients with dedicated commitment. Our aim is to provide value added services and to excel in these activities on a cost effective basis.

Over the years, we have built professional strength, competitive efficiency, and harmonious working relationships to assist our clients.

Amboseli Professional Associates Pvt. Ltd (Amboseli HR) is founded in 1993 by Dr. A.D Monteiro who was United Nations Industrial Development Adviser for many years & Yeshvir Monteiro who is a Business Management specialist educated in prestigious universities in the United States.

Amboseli is managed by Executive Director Mrs. Saritha Uday with a team of young professionals.
