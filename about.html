Who We Are

A legacy of excellence in HR services

Amboseli Professional Associates Pvt. Ltd, (AMBOSELI HR) is owned, managed and operated by a group of professionals with working experience in large industrial establishments such as the Tata Group, commercial and development banking, United Nations Organization, and Commonwealth Fund for Technical Co-operation.

AMBOSELI HR, as a group has wide spectrum of experience, supported by a group of young and dynamic persons with good knowledge of the present status of H.R. in different companies and the fast emerging H.R. requirements in the diversified and growing economy of the future.
